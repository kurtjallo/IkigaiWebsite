    The 7 pillars (in order):

    3. **Operational Architecture** -- Systems that sustain, not just survive. Designing operational infrastructure that supports growth without burning out your people. Outcomes: streamlined workflows, clear SOPs, resource optimization. Included: operational assessment, process mapping, workflow redesign, capacity planning.
    4. **Program Architecture** -- Impact by design, not by accident. Structuring programs for measurable community impact with built-in evaluation. Outcomes: logic model alignment, outcome measurement framework, program sustainability plan. Included: program review, logic model development, evaluation framework, stakeholder feedback systems.
    5. **Leadership Architecture** -- Leaders who build leaders. Developing leadership pipelines and cultures that sustain organizations beyond any single leader. Outcomes: leadership development plan, mentorship framework, succession readiness. Included: leadership assessment, executive coaching, leadership development program design, team alignment sessions.
    6. **Accountability Architecture** -- What gets measured gets strengthened. Building transparent reporting and accountability systems that demonstrate impact to funders and communities. Outcomes: performance dashboard, reporting framework, stakeholder accountability plan. Included: KPI development, reporting system design, board reporting templates, funder reporting alignment.
    7. **Community Architecture** -- Organizations rooted in the communities they serve. Designing authentic community engagement strategies that move beyond consultation to genuine partnership. Outcomes: community engagement strategy, partnership framework, feedback integration systems. Included: community asset mapping, stakeholder analysis, engagement strategy development, partnership agreements.
