# Ikigai Consulting Group Website

## What This Is

A premium, authority-positioning website for Ikigai Consulting Group, an organizational consulting firm founded by Nilda Bastone. The site serves as the digital presence that positions Ikigai as the go-to organizational architect for purpose-driven organizations in Ontario's NGO ecosystem. Six pages designed to establish credibility, showcase the proprietary Ikigai Architecture Model, and convert mission-driven leaders into consulting clients.

## Core Value

When a nonprofit leader or board member lands on this site, they must immediately feel: "These people are serious, credible, and exactly who I need." Authority positioning above all else.

## Context

- **Client:** Nilda Bastone, founder of Ikigai Consulting Group
- **Market:** Ontario's NGO and nonprofit ecosystem -- charities, social service agencies, faith-based organizations, women-led and justice-centered initiatives, boards and executive teams
- **Competitive positioning:** "Organizational Architects" -- not generic consultants, but structural architects who design governance, strategy, operations, programs, leadership, accountability, and community systems
- **Proprietary IP:** The Ikigai Architecture Model (trademarked) with 7 pillars and a 4-phase flow: Blueprint > Build > Strengthen > Sustain
- **The 7 Pillars:** Strategic Architecture, Governance Architecture, Operational Architecture, Program Architecture, Leadership Architecture, Accountability Architecture, Community Architecture
- **Tone:** Sophisticated, confident, structured, human-centered. No corporate fluff. Clean and powerful.
- **Design direction:** Premium and elevated. Think architectural firm website, not generic nonprofit consultant. White space is intentional. Every element earns its place.

## Constraints

- **Design:** Must use Hunter Green (#355E3B or similar), Gold (#D4A843 or similar), Black, and White as the color palette
- **Typography:** Serif font for headlines (authority), clean sans-serif for body text
- **Brand:** Ikigai Architecture Model is trademarked IP -- must include TM symbol where referenced
- **Imagery:** Architectural line drawings and pillar motifs -- no stock photos of smiling people in boardrooms
- **Git:** No Claude co-author attribution in commits, pushes, or PRs
